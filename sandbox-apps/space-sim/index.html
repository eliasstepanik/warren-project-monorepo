Lone Orbit

One ship. One star. Every burn bends your orbit, and gravity never forgets.

THE DEMO

Click the screen to focus it. Keys only work while it is focused. Press R to reset.

    FEATURES

    • REAL GRAVITY

      Your ship feels the pull of the star, every planet and the moon, all at once.

    • PLANETS ON RAILS

      Three planets and a moon follow true elliptical Kepler orbits.

    • SEE THE FUTURE

      A dotted line predicts your path, including where the planets will be.

    • TIME WARP

      Skip ahead up to 64x. The physics stays stable at every speed.

    CONTROLS

    Keys only work while the game screen is focused. A keyboard is needed: the demo has no touch controls. W A S D or the arrow keys fly, comma and period change time warp, plus and minus zoom, P pauses, R resets. F toggles fullscreen, B toggles immersive mode.

    A D / ← →Rotate
    W / ↑Thrust forward
    S / ↓Retro thrust
    , . / Q ESlower / faster time warp (engines off while warping)
    + - / wheelZoom
    PPause / resume
    F / F11Fullscreen
    BImmersive mode (game fills the page)
    RReset
    Space / FFire (multiplayer)
    Tab (hold)Scoreboard (multiplayer)

    Tip: burn along your heading to raise the far side of your orbit. Burn against it to lower it.
